How This Works

A general grammar check, not an official exam. No time limit.

You'll answer 48 multiple-choice questions that start easy and gradually get harder. Many questions test more than one grammar point at once, just like real English does. At the end you'll see an approximate CEFR level (A1 to C1), a rough IELTS guide, and a personalised list of lessons to work on first.
  • No feedback until the end. That keeps the result accurate. You can change an answer any time before you finish.
  • Choose "Not sure" instead of guessing. It counts as a wrong answer, but it gives a more honest level and a better study plan.
  • It's an estimate. Grammar is only one part of English, so treat the level as a guide, not a certificate.
  • Retake it any time. The options are shuffled each attempt, so try again after you've studied.
