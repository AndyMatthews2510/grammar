Using Articles

Three small words with a surprisingly big impact on meaning.

A dog was barking. The dog next door kept me awake all night.

What Are Articles?

Articles go before a noun to show whether it is specific or general, known or unknown.

English has two types: the indefinite article (a / an) for general or first-mentioned nouns, and the definite article (the) for specific or already-known nouns. Sometimes no article is used at all — called the zero article.
a / an
Indefinite Article
Use before singular countable nouns that are non-specific or mentioned for the first time.

a = before consonant sounds
an = before vowel sounds
"I saw a bird." (not a specific bird)
"She is an engineer."
"It was a unique idea." (u = /j/ sound)
the
Definite Article
Use when both speaker and listener know which one: second mention, unique things, superlatives, specific places.
"Pass me the salt." (specific)
"The sun is very hot."
"She's the best in class."
"The Amazon is enormous."
∅ zero
Zero Article (no article)
No article with: plural/uncountable nouns in general, most proper nouns, meals, languages, sports, school subjects.
"∅ Dogs are loyal." (in general)
"∅ Water is essential."
"I had ∅ lunch at noon."
"She speaks ∅ French."
"He plays ∅ tennis."
⚠️ Common Traps
It's the sound, not the letter: a hour → an hour (silent h — vowel sound /aʊ/)
Sound again: an university → a university (u = /j/ — consonant sound)
Some countries take "the": United States, Netherlands → the United States, the Netherlands
🔑 Quick test: Specific and known → the. General/first mention, singular → a/an. General, plural or uncountable → no article.

More Rules to Know

These situations catch out even advanced learners. Study each pattern carefully.

🌍 Geographic Names

The article depends on the type of place — not its size or importance.

Use THE
Rivers: the Thames, the Amazon
Mountain ranges: the Alps, the Rockies
Oceans & seas: the Pacific, the Red Sea
Deserts: the Sahara, the Gobi
Groups of islands: the Maldives, the Philippines
Countries with United / Republic / Kingdom:
  the UK, the USA, the Czech Republic
No Article (∅)
Individual mountains: Mount Everest, Ben Nevis
Individual lakes: Lake Baikal, Lake Victoria
Continents: Asia, Africa, Europe
Most countries: France, Thailand, Japan
Cities & towns: Bangkok, Paris, Tokyo
Streets & roads: Oxford Street, Fifth Avenue

📡 Shared or Unique Things in Society

Things that everyone in a society shares, or that are unique, take the.

"I heard it on the radio."  ·  "Let me check the internet."
"She loves going to the cinema / the theatre."
"Call the police!"  ·  "The government announced new measures."
"We must protect the environment."

🏥 Institution (Purpose) vs Building (Location)

With certain nouns, no article = using the place for its intended purpose; the = the physical building.

No article — the purpose
"She is in ∅ hospital." (as a patient)
"He is at ∅ school." (studying)
"He went to ∅ prison." (sentenced)
"I'm in ∅ bed." (sleeping)
The — the physical place
"I went to the hospital to visit her."
"I drove past the school."
"She works in the prison." (as staff)
"The cat is on the bed." (specific bed)

🎯 General vs Specific

The same noun can take different articles depending on whether you mean it in general or a specific example.

"∅ Music makes me happy." "The music at that concert was incredible."
"∅ Cats are independent animals." "The cats next door keep me awake."
"I love ∅ history." "The history of this city is fascinating."
Red = general (no article).   Green = specific (the).
